Tired of constantly running out of storage for your photos and videos? Or maybe you're not a fan of paying for cloud storage every month?

You might be surprised by how easy it is to build your own home server. With some affordable hardware and a little bit of setup, you can create your own personal cloud and keep your files under your control.

## Prerequisites

1. **An old computer** — preferably with at least a quad-core processor, 8 GB of RAM, and an SSD for the operating system.

2. **Additional storage** — an HDD or SSD with at least 500 GB of storage to get started.

3. **A USB flash drive (8 GB or larger)** — to create the Ubuntu Server installation media.

4. **An Ethernet connection** — recommended for a more reliable connection and faster file transfers.

5. **Basic computer knowledge** — you don't need to be a Linux expert; we'll go through the setup step by step.
